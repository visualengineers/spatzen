# A-Frame
This folder is a very simple test for the A-Frame 3D-Web-Framework (https://github.com/aframevr/aframe#builds)

## Goal
This application uses a Hiro marker to represent a surface or an area that has a grey sign on top of it. (Imagine the marker as a table surface and the panel as a sign for example.) The sign is placed directly at the marker. 

## How to test it
- Open the index.html, accept camera access and open the marker image in this folder on your phone. 
- Place the marker anywhere in the camera detection area and look at the screen.

## Technology used
- A-Frame for the panel and the text
- AR.js for hiro marker detection and camera access
